What I Do

Mortgages for Every
Stage of Life

01
First-Time Home Buyers

Buying your first home is one of the biggest decisions of your life. I'll guide you through every step — from pre-approval to closing — with clear advice and no surprises.

02
Mortgage Renewals

Don't just sign your bank's renewal offer. I shop 50+ lenders at renewal time so you're not leaving money on the table when your term expires.

03
Refinancing

Access your home equity, consolidate high-interest debt, fund renovations, or lower your rate. I structure the solution that makes the most financial sense.

04
Investment Properties

Growing your real estate portfolio requires strategic financing. I help investors structure deals that maximize leverage while keeping cash flow healthy.

05
New Construction

Building a new home in Simcoe County? I specialize in construction financing and understand the local market, timelines, and builder requirements.

06
Self-Employed Mortgages

Banks routinely decline self-employed applicants. I know which lenders work with non-traditional income and get approvals that others can't.
